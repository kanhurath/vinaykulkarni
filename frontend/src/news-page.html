What If Everything We Knew Was Built on Borrowed Assumptions?

What if everything we thought we knew about success, progress, happiness, and even health was built upon borrowed assumptions — mental constructs we never consciously chose? This is not a philosophical exercise. This is the ground beneath our feet.

The Bhāratīya worldview rests upon a sophisticated understanding of reality that cannot be reduced to religious belief or cultural practice. Dharma is not religion in the Western sense — it is the cosmic law that governs all existence, from the movement of galaxies to the beating of a human heart.

The Pañcakoṣa Model and the Sustainability Crisis

Our current sustainability crisis has a simple diagnosis: the whole world began operating in the Artha-Kāma plane and forgot Dharma — the harmonizing principle — and Mokṣa — the liberating principle. Unlimited desires. Limited natural resources.

"One planet is not enough to satiate the untenable greed of a humanity operating without inner restraint. Chitta Shuddhi is the need of the hour."

Every aspect of traditional life — the food we ate, the temples we built, the cities we designed — was crafted so that even the most ordinary person was slowly moved from the Annamaya toward the Ānandamaya koṣa. Day by day. Task by task.
